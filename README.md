# DepositProof

The paper trail that gets your deposit back.

Security deposits come back when the evidence is boring and dated. DepositProof walks you through each area of the rental as you move out - condition notes with timestamps - tracks the legal deposit-return deadline after your move-out date, and generates a landlord-ready condition report you can paste into any email.

## Use it

Open `index.html` for the landing page, or go straight to `app.html`.

Everything runs client-side; your log is stored in the browser's localStorage. No account, no server.

## Files

- `index.html` - landing page
- `app.html` - the app
- `engine.js` - pure deadline + report math, shared by the app and tests
